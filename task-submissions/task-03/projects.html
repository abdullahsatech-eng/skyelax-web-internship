One concept for each of our services, showing the thinking behind the solution.

Website Development

Local Retail Website Concept

Objective
Help a small neighbourhood shop present its products and opening details online.
Solution
A responsive multi-page website with clear navigation, a product overview and a contact page.
Intended outcome
Customers can find essential information quickly on any device.
UI/UX Design

Booking Experience Concept

Objective
Make appointment booking easier for a service business and its customers.
Solution
A simplified step-by-step booking flow with clear labels, helpful error messages and accessible form design.
Intended outcome
Fewer abandoned bookings caused by confusing steps.
Web Applications

Client Dashboard Concept

Objective
Give clients one place to view and track their service requests.
Solution
A browser-based dashboard with a request list, status indicators and search.
Intended outcome
Less time spent on status enquiries for both clients and staff.
Business Automation

Order and Invoice Workflow Concept

Objective
Reduce repetitive manual steps between receiving an order and sending an invoice.
Solution
An automated workflow that collects order details, prepares an invoice draft and notifies the team for approval.
Intended outcome
More consistent records and less repeated data entry.
